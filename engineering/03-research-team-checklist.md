# Research Team Checklist

Complete this checklist before handing a university to Codex. Use the entry cycle and applicant pool on every note. If an item cannot be found, record the search performed and the unresolved gap rather than filling it from assumption.

## Course and policy sources

- [ ] Official course page
- [ ] Official admissions policy or selection procedure
- [ ] Current entry year and publication/retrieval dates
- [ ] UCAS course identity and code
- [ ] Official UCAT policy
- [ ] Archived official page or document where the live page omits a cycle-specific rule

## Academic eligibility

- [ ] GCSE requirements, subject rules, scoring, and resit treatment
- [ ] A-Level grades, required subjects, excluded combinations, and resit treatment
- [ ] Practical endorsement requirement
- [ ] Scottish Higher and Advanced Higher requirements, if applicable
- [ ] IB total, Higher Level, subject, and resit requirements
- [ ] Graduate or prior-degree rules, if applicable
- [ ] Access, foundation, transfer, or other supported route rules, if applicable
- [ ] International qualification requirements from official country or qualification pages
- [ ] English-language requirements where they affect eligibility

## Applicant pools and selection

- [ ] All applicant pools and the rules assigning applicants to them
- [ ] Home and international pool differences
- [ ] Contextual or widening-participation policy and required evidence
- [ ] Special programmes or guaranteed-interview routes
- [ ] Academic scoring model, components, weights, caps, and tie-breaks
- [ ] Interview selection model and order of operations
- [ ] Current UCAT cognitive-total treatment
- [ ] Current SJT treatment
- [ ] Published UCAT thresholds, cutoffs, deciles, or ranking method
- [ ] Interview format and current-cycle details

## Historical evidence

- [ ] Historical UCAT thresholds or interview-selection ranges, labelled by cycle and pool
- [ ] Historical interview data
- [ ] International historical data
- [ ] Contextual historical data where available
- [ ] Number of applications by cycle and pool
- [ ] Number of interviews by cycle and pool
- [ ] Number of offers by cycle and pool
- [ ] Number of places by cycle and pool
- [ ] Definitions and denominators for every statistic
- [ ] FOI evidence where official published evidence is insufficient

Offer-selection information may be retained as optional research notes only. It is not required for ApplySmart readiness and must not be framed as a product prediction.

## Evidence record

For each finding, provide:

- [ ] Source title and direct URL
- [ ] Publisher
- [ ] Publication date or retrieval date
- [ ] Entry cycle
- [ ] Applicable applicant pool
- [ ] The exact fact or rule supported
- [ ] Whether the evidence is official policy, official data, or attributable FOI evidence
- [ ] Any conflict with another source and how it was resolved
- [ ] Any unresolved ambiguity or evidence gap

## Forbidden evidence sources

Do not use the following as evidence:

- Reddit;
- forums;
- blogs;
- unofficial summaries;
- The Student Room.

The Student Room may be retained only as clearly labelled, non-evidence context that does not support a rule, score, threshold, confidence judgement, or readiness decision.

## Handoff gate

Before handoff, confirm:

- [ ] Every relevant onboarding section is complete, not applicable, or logged as an unresolved gap.
- [ ] Official evidence supports every proposed executable rule.
- [ ] Historical observations are separated from current policy.
- [ ] Pool-specific evidence has not been generalised to other pools.
- [ ] No missing rule or statistic has been guessed.
- [ ] The evidence pack is sufficient to map the university into the frozen architecture.
