# University of Bristol – MB ChB Medicine
## Contextual Admissions Requirements (Official Source Only)

**Course code note:** Bristol's standard 5-year Medicine course also uses UCAS code **A100** — the same code as Birmingham and Aston. Check the institution code to distinguish them. Bristol's widening-participation Gateway route uses code **A108**.

**Sources:**
1. Course page: https://www.bristol.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/2027/medicine/mb-chb-medicine/
2. Contextual offers page: https://www.bristol.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/entry-requirements-qualifications/contextual-offers/

**Applies to:** 2027 entry (contextual eligibility criteria explicitly stated as applying to applicants applying from September 2026 for 2027 entry — reconfirm each cycle, as the university states criteria are "subject to change")

## 1. A-Level Offers Comparison

| Offer type | Grades required | Subjects |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard offer** | AAA | Chemistry, and either Biology, Physics, Mathematics, or Further Mathematics |
| **Contextual offer** | **ABB**, with **A specifically in Chemistry** and **B in one of** Biology/Physics/Mathematics/Further Mathematics | As above |
| **Graduate offer** (for reference — not contextual) | 2:1 degree + BBB at A-level | Chemistry, and either Biology, Physics, Mathematics or Further Mathematics |

This is a reduction of **up to two grades** below standard entry, per Bristol's general contextual offer policy.

## 2. Who Qualifies for the Contextual Offer

Per the official Contextual Offers page, an applicant may be eligible if they meet **all** of the following baseline conditions:
- Have **home fee status**
- Are studying **A-Levels and/or the International Baccalaureate**
- Apply via **UCAS**

...**and** at least **one** of the following applies:

### a) Attended an "aspiring state school or college"
- Defined by Bristol as a school/college with **low average attainment and progression to higher education statistics**
- Determined using the **DfE / devolved government data** and Bristol's own published list
- If currently at school/college: must appear on the **current year's Aspiring State Schools list**
- If you've left school/college: must have attended a listed school for the most recent academic year (2025/26 for 2026-list purposes)
- Bristol notes inclusion on this list is **not a judgement of institutional quality** — many listed schools are rated "outstanding" in other respects
- Determined via the "applying from" school code on the UCAS form (or education history, if applying independently)

### b) Live in an IMD Quintile 1 or 2 area
- Bristol uses **Indices of Multiple Deprivation (IMD)** data (distinct from POLAR4, which other universities use)
- IMD ranks neighbourhoods from most to least deprived
- Home postcode in the **top 40% most deprived areas** (quintile 1 or 2) qualifies
- Based on the **home address on the UCAS form** at point of application

### c) Completed a University of Bristol widening participation programme
Qualifying programmes named on the official page:
- Access to Bristol
- Bristol Scholars *(note: this one leads to a **tailored offer**, not the standard contextual offer)*
- Discover Bristol
- Insight into Bristol summer school
- Next Step Bristol
- Virtual Summer School

### d) Spent time in care
- **Three months or more** in local authority care qualifies
- Applicant ticks the relevant box on the UCAS form
- If the UCAS reference doesn't independently confirm this, Bristol will request supporting evidence (e.g. a letter from the local authority, school, or other organisation)

### e) Eligible for Free School Meals (FSM)
- Eligibility **during secondary education** qualifies
- Bristol cross-checks via the **National Pupil Database** (England) or equivalent databases for Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland
- Alternatively, a confirming letter from the applicant's school is accepted

**Important exclusions stated on the official page:** Contextual offers do **not** apply to Bristol's CertHE Foundation courses or its Gateway courses (A108).

**Note on assessment:** No separate application form is required — eligibility is assessed automatically from the UCAS application. Applicants meeting FSM or care-experience criteria may also qualify for a **guaranteed offer or guaranteed interview** (a related, separate scheme).

## 3. GCSE Profile Requirements (All Applicants — No Separate Contextual GCSE Tier Published)

- **Advanced numeracy requirement**: grade 7/A in GCSE Mathematics (or equivalent)
- **Standard literacy requirement**: grade 4/C in GCSE English (or equivalent)

No distinct, lower contextual GCSE threshold is published on Bristol's Medicine entry requirements page — the ABB contextual adjustment applies specifically to **A-Levels** (and IB, see Section 5).

---

## 4. UCAT Requirement

- All applicants must sit the **UCAT** (formerly UKCAT)
- Bursaries are available to cover the cost of taking the UCAT (details via the UCAT website)
- The **combined score from all subtests, excluding Situational Judgement**, is used to select applicants for interview
- **For 2026 entry**, the final UCAT threshold score to be invited to interview was **2240 for Home applicants** and **2270 for Overseas applicants** — Bristol states this threshold changes year on year
- No separate, lower UCAT threshold is published specifically for contextual applicants on the official course page (third-party sources report a historically lower "contextual" interview cutoff in past cycles, but this is not confirmed on Bristol's own current page, so it is excluded here per your official-source-only request)

## 5. International Baccalaureate — Standard vs Contextual

| Offer type | Points | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | 36 points overall | 18 at Higher Level, including 6,6 at HL in Chemistry and one of Biology/Physics/Maths (Analysis & Approaches or Applications & Interpretations) |
| **Contextual** | **32 points overall** | 16 at Higher Level, including **6 at HL in Chemistry** and **5 at HL** in one of Biology/Physics/Maths |

## 6. Selection Process Notes

- UCAS application deadline for Medicine: **15 October**
- Applicants must be **18 years of age (or above) on 1 September** of the year they enter the programme
- Interviews take place **November to April** following application submission
- Work experience is **not a strict requirement** but is encouraged
- Full selection methodology is published in Bristol's official **Admissions Statement** (PDF, updated annually): https://www.bristol.ac.uk/study/media/undergraduate/admissions-statements/2027/medicine.pdf
- Extenuating circumstances are handled via a separate published policy: https://www.bristol.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/after-you-apply/your-application/extenuating-circumstances/

## 7. Related Widening-Participation Route: Gateway to Medicine (A108)

Distinct from the standard contextual offer above — a separate one-year Gateway programme exists for students who do not meet standard academic entry criteria:
- Typical offer: **BBC**, including B in Biology or Chemistry
- Applicants with predicted/achieved **AAB or above** (or an A in Chemistry) are **not eligible** for this route — it is reserved for those below the standard threshold
- Open to applicants from **specific schools/colleges** and/or those who have spent **3+ months in care**
- Successful completion leads to automatic progression onto the 5-year MB ChB
- Additional eligibility criteria apply — check before applying: https://www.bristol.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/entry-requirements-qualifications/gateway-eligibility/
